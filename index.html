Privacy Policy for Vidnux

Effective Date: 13 September 2026

Vidnux ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Vidnux mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user information and data.

1. Information Collection

We respect your privacy.

  • We do not collect personal information
  • No login or account required
  • No user tracking
  • No personal data stored on servers

2. Local Data Storage

All data such as downloaded videos, audio files, and locked media are stored only on your device.

We do not upload or access your personal files.

3. Permissions We Use

  • Storage / Media Access: To read and save videos & audio
  • Internet: Required for downloading content
  • Network State: To check connectivity
  • Notifications: To show download progress
  • Foreground Service: For background download & playback
  • Wake Lock: Prevent interruption during downloads
  • Vibration: For user feedback

These permissions are only used for app functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

We use limited third-party services:

  • Render Server: To process video download requests
  • Cloudflare Pages: For hosting updates

No personal data is collected or stored by these services through our app.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data, your privacy risk is minimal. All files remain under your control.

6. User Control & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data.

  • Delete files manually anytime
  • Uninstall app to remove all data

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is safe for general users.

8. Content Usage Disclaimer

Vidnux does not host any content.

Users are responsible for the content they download or access.

The app should only be used to download content that is publicly available or where the user has permission.

9. Legal Compliance

We aim to comply with:

  • GDPR
  • CCPA
  • COPPA
